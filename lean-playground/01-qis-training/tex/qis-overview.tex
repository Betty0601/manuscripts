\section{QIS Phased Training Track}\label{sec:qis-training}

The \texttt{01-qis-training} project follows the phased training programme
built around Nielsen \& Chuang, \emph{Quantum Computation and Quantum
Information}~\cite{NielsenChuang2010}, and targets the skills needed for
security reductions in the quantum random oracle model
(QROM)~\cite{BDFLSZ2011QROM} of ML-KEM~\cite{FIPS203} and
ML-DSA~\cite{FIPS204}. It is split into two tracks:

\begin{description}
  \item[Formal mathematics (\texttt{formal-math})] Lean/Mathlib
        formalisation of the mathematics used in game-based reductions:
        complex vector spaces, the spectral theorem, tensor products and
        partial traces, and discrete probability and expectation.
  \item[Numeric and circuit verification (\texttt{numeric-circuit})]
        Fixed-precision numeric checks, structured descriptions of
        quantum circuits, and cross-checks of Wolfram numerics against
        exact Lean statements.
\end{description}
